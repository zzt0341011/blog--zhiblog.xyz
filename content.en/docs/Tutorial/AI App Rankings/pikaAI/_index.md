## The Story of Pika AI

[Pika AI](https://pika.art/try) is a successful case in the AI startup field. The founder is a Chinese entrepreneur named Wenjing Guo, a highly accomplished academic with a striking presence. She studied at Hangzhou No. 2 High School and later attended Harvard University. After graduation, she co-founded Pika with classmates from Stanford University. The company focuses on AI video generation and is currently valued at approximately $400 million, headquartered in California, USA.
