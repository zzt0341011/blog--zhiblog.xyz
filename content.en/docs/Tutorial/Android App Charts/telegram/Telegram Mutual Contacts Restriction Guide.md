When using [Telegram](https://telegram.org/), you may encounter the following message:

**Sorry, you can only send messages to mutual contacts at the moment.**

This is a restriction mechanism set by Telegram to prevent spam and advertising.

![telegram1--001.jpg](https://zhiblog.xyz/img/telegram1--001.jpg)

### 1. Click “Learn more” to open the official bot

Open the official anti-spam bot: [@SpamBot](https://t.me/SpamBot)

![telegram1--002.jpg](https://zhiblog.xyz/img/telegram1--002.jpg)

### 2. Click Start at the bottom

- If your Telegram account has no restrictions, you will see a message similar to this:

![telegram1--003.jpg](https://zhiblog.xyz/img/telegram1--003.jpg)

- If your account is under some restriction, a different message will appear.
- Simply follow the prompts to submit an appeal.
- Tell Telegram that you encountered an error.

![telegram1--004.jpg](https://zhiblog.xyz/img/telegram1--004.jpg)

- Next, the bot will ask whether you have sent spam messages to others.
- **Do not admit to sending spam.**
- Clearly state that you have never sent any spam messages.
- This usually removes the mutual contacts restriction on Telegram.
