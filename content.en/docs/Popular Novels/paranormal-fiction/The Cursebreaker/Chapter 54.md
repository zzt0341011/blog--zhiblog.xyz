The house began to shake like a starved beast swinging its terrible claws. Juexing pulled Orange toward the exit. Instantly a vine crawled across the floor and wrapped around her left ankle. He spun, snatched up a chair, smashed the vine in two with a heavy blow, then yanked hard and leapt through the window together with her.

Half a length of vine still clung to Orange’s ankle, still alive and attempting to pierce her skin. Suddenly she felt a needle-like pain. A moment later the severed piece lost its vitality, slipped from her ankle, and lay faded on the ground.

Juexing crouched at once to examine the ankle. Strangely, there was no wound at all. Did Orange possess some special natural serum?

“It’s nothing,” he said, standing up.

The words had barely left his mouth when the entire house seemed crushed by enormous pressure. With a single *bang* it shattered into pieces, sending up a cloud of green smoke. Within the smoke a gigantic vine plant brought the whole structure down and vanished into the ruins.

“What on earth was that?” Orange closed her eyes and rested her head against Juexing’s shoulder.

“The painting—I finally understand what the painting means!” Realization struck him.

“What do we do next?” She too understood that catastrophe was about to fall.

“The root of everything should lie inside that painting. We have to obtain it and burn it.”

“Even if we do, that may not eliminate this terrifying plant. I feel as if it is growing from the earth itself.” What she said was something Juexing had already considered. If this was not a curse but a genuine trial that truly existed, the situation would become far more troublesome.

At that moment he suddenly recalled the words Shang Xuan had spoken the night before: “It won’t be that simple…”

“Are you all right, Juexing?” Orange looked at him as he stared into space.

“I’m fine. It seems we need to visit the gallery first. Last night I discovered a tunnel. Perhaps…”  

As he spoke, several helicopters appeared in the sky, followed by the rising wail of sirens.

“The whole city is on alert. Its movements are faster than we imagined,” Orange said.

“Go home first and evacuate with your family.”

“I won’t go back. I said I would fight side by side with you. Father and Mother will approve of my choice.” Her voice was firm.

“They’re already evacuating.” Jiaye and Yu Xun ran up and delivered the news.

“You came as well?” Juexing felt both surprise and relief.

“I promised Yu Xun that our two couples would get married together. Heaven itself won’t allow me to desert the battlefield.” Jiaye declared.

At that moment Yu Xun felt he had become especially manly. Perhaps certain men only reveal their true strength when real crisis arrives.

“We have to go to the gallery,” Juexing said.

“Because plant attacks have occurred in different places, the Metropolitan Police Department has issued a mandatory order. Many people are joining the evacuation, and checkpoints have been set up in several areas. Getting into the city center may be difficult.” Jiaye shared the intelligence he had gathered.

“No matter what, we still have to go. The root is very likely inside that tunnel!” Juexing spoke while already calculating possible solutions.

While the four of them were still discussing, Orange’s phone rang. The caller was her classmate—Miyuki, married less than a week. On the other end her voice was panicked and edged with tears. She told Orange that Dou Dou had gone to the gallery that morning and never returned. With no other options she had called Orange for help.

While comforting her, Orange exchanged a glance with Juexing. He said, “We’ll go to Miyuki’s house first, then figure out a way into the gallery.”

With no better plan available they could only begin with the simplest step. Because of the outbreak of strange events not a single vehicle remained on the streets, so the four of them had to walk to Miyuki’s home.

The entire route felt lifeless—an empty-city landscape. High-rises still stood, yet a stealthy green aura already permeated everything, as though the end of the world itself were attempting to seize the city.

After roughly forty minutes of walking they reached Miyuki’s house, a lavishly renovated three-story building. Yet even the most beautiful residence could no longer shine; like the surrounding ordinary houses it possessed no vitality at all.

Inside the vast dwelling only Miyuki remained, clutching a wedding photograph. She seemed completely indifferent to the changes around her and focused solely on finding Dou Dou.

“It’s all right. Perhaps he has already been transferred to a safe place,” Orange said gently.

“I don’t know why this is happening… what on earth is going on…” Miyuki held the photograph tightly and curled up on the sofa, her terrified appearance deeply unsettling.

Juexing glanced at the wall. Beside the clock hung a picture frame containing Dou Dou and Miyuki’s wedding photo. Then what was she holding in her arms right now?

He suddenly turned. What Miyuki clutched was not a photograph at all but a flowerpot. Inside it grew a green vine plant, and on the vine bloomed several small black flowers—truly black, almost the color of night. In a single stride Juexing rushed forward, snatched the frame from her arms, and smashed it hard against the floor. The instant the glass shattered, a vine as thick as a wrist shot out of the frame and lunged at him like a water snake. Jiaye seized a chair from the floor and brought it down heavily on the frame. The green vine seemed struck by the blow; it swayed, then collapsed in front of Juexing and withered on the ground.

“This place is not safe for long. Leave now!” Juexing ordered. Orange and Yu Xun quickly supported the still-dazed Miyuki and led her to the Mercedes parked outside. Miyuki kept crying for the wedding photograph. Juexing pushed Jiaye and said, “You drive. I’ll get the frame.”

Jiaye was about to refuse, but Juexing glared at him; he had no choice but to run to the car.

Juexing turned and re-entered the house. By now many vine plants were already crawling toward him like snakes. He quickly grabbed a stool, climbed onto it, and took down the frame hanging on the wall. Just as he prepared to jump, vines wrapped around both of his feet and rooted him in place.

Jiaye glanced at the house. Through the window he saw the green plants slowly gathering. Five minutes had already passed and Juexing had not emerged. He steeled himself and started the engine.

“Where is Juexing?” Only then did Orange notice he was not in the car.

“There isn’t room for so many people.” Jiaye did not know why he said the words.

“Let me out. Juexing must still be inside.” The moment Orange’s hand touched the door handle Jiaye engaged the central lock, preventing her from leaving.

“Are you insane? Open the door!” She yanked at the handle with no effect. Seeing Orange’s rising agitation only increased Miyuki’s terror. Jiaye shifted gears, stepped on the accelerator, and shot out of the parking area.

Orange pounded on the window glass. Yu Xun could no longer bear it. “Jiaye, stop the car! We can’t leave Juexing behind!” He had no choice but to brake. Orange flung the door open and sprinted back, yet the structure before her was no longer an ordinary house—it had become an enormous green monster.

Juexing was bound hand and foot by vines and suspended in mid-air. Although the terrifying plants posed no immediate lethal threat, he could not free himself. Within a few days he would simply starve to death.

Orange smashed open a fire-cabinet with a brick, seized a red fire axe, and began swinging it, severing the coiling vines one after another. The plant seemed to register the attack and shifted its target toward her, yet every tendril that came within a centimeter of her body was cut short by the axe. The severed pieces still dripping green mucus continued to crawl toward her, but the instant they touched her skin they behaved as though they had met fire—sliding off and withering on the ground.

She cleared a path with continuous swings of the axe and soon saw Juexing hanging in the air, still holding Dou Dou and Miyuki’s wedding photograph.

“You broke your promise—unforgivable!” All of her rage gathered on the sharp edge of the axe. Wherever the blade passed, only decay remained. In moments she had cut through every vine binding him. Juexing broke free and shook off the remaining tendrils.

The moment he looked up, a vine bearing a black six-petaled flower lunged at the back of Orange’s head. He shouted, “Watch out!” She spun and brought the axe down from above, yet the vine’s force was greater; it knocked the axe from her hands. The spinning blade cut through the air, striking sparks. Juexing calculated height and angle, then pulled Orange outward at a run. More vines still pursued them. Just as the plants prepared to seize them again a tremendous explosion ended the nightmare.

The whirling axe had struck a liquefied-gas cylinder. The sparks ignited the gas. In an instant flames roared skyward, setting the entire house ablaze. The intense heat used the plants themselves as fuel and rapidly expanded, swallowing the terrible vegetation with its bright body…

Unable to gauge the force of the blast, Juexing protected Orange beneath him as best he could. Fortunately the flying glass fragments were few; only a few light cuts marked his back. Those wounds, however, could never compare with the courage Orange had shown in returning to save him.

She trembled and held him tightly. Without her insistence the two of them might already have been separated forever.

She cried and beat against his chest, repeating, “You lied to me… you lied to me…”

Seeing her so heartbroken, Juexing decided that no matter what happened he would never again leave Orange behind. Even if they had to die, they would die together.

Fire is merciless, yet when facing this ferocious plant it showed no fear and continued to burn with its proper heat. Jiaye drove toward the art gallery. Someone was already waiting there, prepared to reveal the true identity of the tunnel.

### Character Notes

**Qingmu Chengzi (Orange)**  
Refuses to abandon Juexing. She breaks open a fire cabinet, seizes a red axe, and single-handedly cuts him free from the living vines. Her skin itself appears toxic to the plant force.

**Luo Juexing**  
Understands the meaning of the Soul-Binding Pagoda painting. After being rescued he vows never again to leave Orange behind, even in death.

**Huang Jiaye**  
Initially drives away under pressure, then stops when confronted. His promise to marry Yu Xun becomes the reason he refuses to desert the fight.

**Miyuki**  
Still clutching what she believes is a wedding photograph—actually a potted vine bearing black flowers—until the illusion is shattered.

**The Green Vine Force**  
Continues to expand, turning houses into living traps. When attacked by Orange’s axe and finally by fire, it is temporarily destroyed at this location, yet the true root remains at the gallery.
