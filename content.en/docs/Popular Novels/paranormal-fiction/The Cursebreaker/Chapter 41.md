“I saw Shang Xuan,” Orange said. “He told me to make sure you stop looking into Wanting.”

“The only reason I have to keep watching this case is Little Bell,” Juexing answered.

“What about her?” Orange looked puzzled.

The previous night Little Bell had pounded on Juexing’s door, completely drunk. He had never seen her drink before, let alone walk as if performing a drunken boxing routine.

He helped her into the guest room. Before he could even let go she threw up all over him. After he finally managed to pour sobering tea down her throat she still refused to sleep. Instead she sat across from him and began talking about something that had happened at the Tiantianhao Supermarket.

“I never had the courage to do something like that… but every time I thought about her being all lovey-dovey with Shang Xuan I felt sick. I know that in his eyes I’m still just a child. I told myself I’d confess once I got into university, but I couldn’t wait any longer. That sister named Wanting has taken over his heart!”

The words tumbled out in one breath. Then she collapsed onto the table and began to sob.

Juexing let out a long sigh. He could not understand what place Shang Xuan occupied in Little Bell’s heart, but he knew that loving someone this deeply required a foundation—or that something significant had once happened between them—to reach this state.

“It’s all right. There are plenty of other men in the world. You don’t have to fixate on Shang Xuan.” He had thought the light remark might ease her pain. Instead it had the opposite effect. She lifted her tear-streaked face and shouted at him:

“What do you know? If one day Orange saw you with someone else, wouldn’t she be just as heartbroken?”

Juexing was left speechless. He could only manage a foolish smile.

“I don’t know why I did it. Call it selfishness or jealousy if you want. I’m terrified that if Shang Xuan finds out I was the one who locked the door and started the fire, he’ll cut me off completely.” She stared out the window, her eyes full of fear and despair, as if she had already seen the ending.

“Did you do something to Wanting?” Juexing asked.

“I saw her tidying things in the third-floor storeroom. On impulse I locked the door from the outside. I didn’t want her to get out easily, so I set a fire in a trash bin on the second floor and triggered the fire alarm…” Her voice cracked with sobs. The sight of her pain made Juexing’s own chest tighten.

“The reason I wanted your help was so that when the time was right I could be with Shang Xuan. Is even this tiny wish of mine not allowed?”

“Have you ever tried telling him how you feel?”

“I’ve hinted at it. He only treats me like a child. But I’m already over eighteen. I’m allowed to fall in love…”

“Maybe…” Juexing managed only two words before she cut him off.

“I only wanted to give her a little punishment. I never thought it would get so serious. When I hid and watched Shang Xuan, frantic with worry, carrying her into the ambulance, I really regretted it!”

…

“That night she couldn’t sleep. She stayed here until dawn. After saying goodbye she left for school looking completely numb.” Juexing finished recounting the previous night’s events. Orange listened with her heart pounding. She could not imagine such a plot coming from a high-school graduate.

“Caring too much about feelings makes it easy to lose yourself. I was like that once—I even thought about giving up. Looking at it this way, our generation’s emotional experiences still can’t match a high-school student’s clarity.” Orange sighed.

Juexing took her hand. “Orange, I don’t care how other people see this. Whether it’s for Shang Xuan or for the curse that’s about to surface, I have to stand as a cursebreaker and accept the challenge.”

“You mean…?” She was beginning to understand.

“The case of Wanting has never had a killer. I’ll be the one to uncover the answer. Even if it costs a friendship in the end, I have to make Shang Xuan understand that meetings and partings are already decided by fate.”

“But the two of you already have friction. If it escalates I’m worried…” Orange pressed her lips together.

“So pretend you never ran into any of this. Stay in the city for now and don’t come back. When it’s over we’ll meet again.”

“No. Why do you always have to carry everything alone? Why do you always treat me like a burden? I can help you. Have you forgotten we said we’d face things together?” She shook off his hand and stood by the window. Night wind lifted the curtain and brushed the tears at the corner of her eye.

“I’m sorry. I shouldn’t have isolated you again. Since you consider Shang Xuan a friend, I’ll help him without hesitation. I’ll set personal grudges aside for now.” Juexing took a step back.

Orange nodded. “Yu Xun and Jiaye already know about this, right? I imagine they’ve joined the search for the truth as well.”

“Right—you’re the counselor for Little Bell’s class. I need you to keep a close eye on her. I’m worried she’ll try something else.”

“Don’t worry. The college entrance exams are almost here. School won’t be giving holidays for a while. I’ll watch her carefully.”

“Good. I’ll call Jiaye over so we can discuss the details.” Juexing dialed the number.

The city looked calm on the surface, yet a faint layer of melancholy hung over it, like streets after rain—muddy yet still carrying the cleanliness of having been washed. After that “accident” Shang Xuan and Wanting’s relationship had grown even closer. He drove her to and from work; in their free time they wandered mountains and rivers, looking for all the world like a pair of lovers lost in happiness.

On the surface they were a fairy-tale couple deeply in love. Yet Shang Xuan could feel a crisis slowly closing in around them, like a giant beehive hanging under the eaves. It looked quiet while the bees flew about on their own business; one careless move and a person could be stung into a swollen mess.

He began gathering information from every angle and gradually uncovered the unsolved case from three years earlier. He knew that if he could grasp the hidden truth behind it, he might be able to go against heaven itself and preserve this relationship.

Behind every case there were always instigators. Those poisonous claws in the shadows would never simply wait to be caught. True or false, they would invent a reason that let them rest easy before they were willing to stop.

Everything was progressing with subtle precision, like starlight that peers into every corner of the night. No matter where a person hid, what they held in their hands, or what they thought, nothing remained completely hidden. It was a little like the film *Infernal Affairs*: you watch me, I watch him, he watches you. None of them truly know one another, yet each holds the others in check—pull one thread and the entire web moves. The frantic planning is not only the scheme of those who claim to act for heaven; it may also be a conspiracy of the most dangerous kind. Will the accident that happened three years ago simply repeat itself three years later?

Unease. The air was thick with every kind of unease—worry, hesitation, reluctance to let go, cold ruthlessness. All of them were faces this world had evolved.

The light in the room went out. After watching Wanting fall asleep with a smile still on her face, Shang Xuan put on a jacket and headed to the seaside park for an appointment. Someone had asked him to meet at the swing area at ten-ten that night.

The second hand passed twelve. Shang Xuan appeared at the swings. Juexing was already waiting; he had arrived five minutes early.

“You’re the one who asked me here?” Shang Xuan said.

“What, now that you have a girlfriend you don’t even recognize me anymore?” Juexing teased.

Shang Xuan gave a cold laugh. “We haven’t been in contact for so long. You’ve changed a lot.”

“Have I? Because I’ve seen more clearly what is worth cherishing and what is just drawing water with a bamboo basket.” Something discordant colored Juexing’s words. Shang Xuan easily guessed he was aiming them at Wanting.

“Spit it out. What do you want? Is there anything left for us to talk about?” Shang Xuan made no effort to hide his impatience.

Juexing turned, took two photographs from his pocket, and handed them over. “This one is Lu Wanting’s cousin, Lu Shengnan. The woman is his wife, Zhuzhu. The man behind them is Tian Shubin—now Lu Shengnan’s representative lawyer.”

Shang Xuan examined the photos, then looked up. “And?”

“I don’t know who leaked the information, but they already know Wanting is still ‘alive.’ They will do everything possible to deal with the two of you. And I know the inheritance case is problematic. It may very well drag out facts that were covered up.”

“You’re helping me?” Shang Xuan studied him with open suspicion.

“I’m not sure if this counts as ‘helping.’ Or put more simply—I’m doing it for one particular person.” A bitter smile touched the corner of Juexing’s mouth. The image of Little Bell from that night floated in his mind like a shadow, harder to erase than any nightmare.

Shang Xuan nodded. “Thanks. But my affairs I’ll handle myself. Just don’t kick me when I’m down.”

“Their network is huge. They might even have underworld connections. Alone you might…” Juexing did not want to finish the sentence. Perhaps he was simply worrying too much.

“I know better than to let my guard down. I’ll keep a close eye on those three. If there’s nothing else, I’ll be going.” Shang Xuan put the photographs away, turned, and left.

What neither of them realized was that their meeting at the seaside park had already been placed under surveillance. Men in black suits, thick-featured and wearing the same side-parted haircuts, stood watching. High-bridged noses supported dark sunglasses—though why anyone would wear sunglasses at night while hiding in the shadows was a question best left unanswered.

It simply felt like the pure, undiluted presence of the underworld.

Juexing watched Shang Xuan’s retreating back, then glanced sideways toward the large tree behind him. Two men in black suits immediately turned and walked away. It seemed a silent contest was about to begin.

### Character Notes

**Luo Juexing**  
Learns the full extent of Little Bell’s jealousy-driven actions. He chooses to confront the curse as a professional, setting personal history with Shang Xuan aside for the sake of one person—and for the truth.

**Little Bell**  
Her drunken confession exposes the storeroom lock and the deliberate fire. Her love for Shang Xuan has turned desperate and self-destructive; regret comes too late to undo the harm.

**Shang Xuan**  
Outwardly enjoying a sweet relationship with Wanting while privately sensing the closing net. He accepts Juexing’s warning but insists on handling the threat alone.

**Qingmu Chengzi (Orange)**  
Refuses to be sidelined. After hearing Little Bell’s story she understands the emotional stakes and insists on remaining part of the fight.

**Lu Shengnan’s Circle**  
Already aware that Wanting is “alive.” Their network is extensive enough that black-suited watchers appear the moment Juexing and Shang Xuan meet.

**The Watchers**  
Silent men in black suits and nighttime sunglasses. Their presence signals that the inheritance conflict has moved from paper into open, physical surveillance.
